# Recruiter Call — Talking Points (Dataminr)

1. **Opening (30s):** "I've built three end-to-end systems where LLM ensembles feed calibrated classifiers with threshold policies — the same shape as your event-detection pipeline. My Red-Team framework runs 850 samples/hour at $0.018/sample with Krippendorff's α = 0.81."

2. **Calibration story:** "ECE 0.0089 after Platt on the cancer classifier. I care about calibration because a threshold decision is only as trustworthy as the probability underneath it — and alerting products live or die on that."

3. **Ensemble reliability:** "92% pairwise correlation across GPT-4o, Claude, and Llama on bias ratings — and where they disagreed, PyMC partial pooling gave me publisher-level credible intervals instead of averaging the disagreement away."

4. **Domain jump:** "None of my projects were news/geo signal, but the architecture generalizes — I'd want to spend the first month benchmarking Dataminr's current stack against a fresh eval design before proposing changes."

5. **Ask:** "Where is the Research org spending most of its time — new-signal onboarding, precision optimization on existing categories, or evaluation infrastructure? I want to hear where the bottleneck actually is."
